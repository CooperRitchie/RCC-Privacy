Privacy Policy for Retained Relief Standby

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Retained Relief Standby ("the app") is an independent tool created by Cooper Ritchie - Ritchie Contracting & Consulting to help retained/relief fire station crews organise standby rostering. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced by Fire and Rescue NSW or any other agency.

What information the app handles

Depending on how you use the app, it may handle the following information:

  • Names of crew members, and their RR/RT hours and allocation history, imported from an Hours of Cover Excel report that you obtain through your own authorised access to your station's rostering system
  • Names, and qualification records (e.g. MD, IH, RCR, PR) that you add or edit yourself within the app
  • Standby opportunity details you enter, such as destination station, start and end times, and required qualifications
  • Your chosen app settings, such as your station profile and whether you use Basic Mode or Memory Mode

Where this information is stored

All of the information above is stored only on your own device, using standard local device storage. It is never uploaded to, or stored on, any server operated by the developer. The developer does not have access to this data, and it is not shared with the developer or any third party for analytics, marketing, or any other purpose.

If you use Basic Mode, none of this information is retained between sessions - it is cleared as soon as you import a new report. If you use Memory Mode, this information is retained on your device between sessions until you clear it yourself.

You can permanently delete all locally stored data at any time using the "Reset App" option in the app's Settings tab, or by uninstalling the app.

The only information sent off your device

To calculate driving distance and travel time between fire stations, the app sends geographic coordinates (the latitude and longitude of the two stations involved) to OpenRouteService (openrouteservice.org), an independent third-party routing service. This is the only network request the app makes.

No names, hours, qualifications, or any other personal information are included in these requests - only station coordinates. OpenRouteService's own handling of this data is governed by their own privacy policy, available at openrouteservice.org.

Sharing standby details

The app lets you choose to share confirmed standby details (station, times, and the confirmed person's name) using your device's own share function, for example to send a text message or WhatsApp message. This action is entirely initiated and controlled by you - the app itself does not transmit this information anywhere, and has no involvement in the message once your device's share sheet opens.

No advertising or analytics

The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

Children's privacy

This app is a workplace scheduling tool intended for use by adult retained/relief firefighting crew members and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
