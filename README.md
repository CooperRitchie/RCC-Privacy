# Privacy Policy for Standby Roster Calculator

*Last updated: October 2026*

Standby Roster Calculator ("the app") is an independent tool created by Cooper Ritchie - Ritchie Contracting & Consulting to help teams organise standby and relief shifts. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced by any agency or organisation.

## What information the app handles

Depending on how you use the app, it may handle the following information:

- Names of people on a roster, and their RR/RT hours and allocation history, taken from a roster report you import or from a roster you create yourself
- Qualifications that you define, and which of them you assign to each person
- Standby details you enter, such as destination, start and end times, how many people are needed, and required qualifications
- The name of your own station or unit, and the names and addresses of any locations you add
- Your chosen settings, such as your rules profile and whether you use Basic Mode or Memory Mode

## Where this information is stored

All of the information above is stored **only on your own device**, using standard local device storage. It is never uploaded to, or stored on, any server operated by the developer. The developer does not have access to this data, and it is not shared with the developer or any third party for analytics, marketing, or any other purpose.

If you use **Basic Mode**, your roster, report and standby information is not retained between sessions. If you use **Memory Mode**, this information is retained on your device between sessions until you clear it yourself. In both modes, app settings (your station name, saved locations, and your qualification list) are kept on your device so you do not need to enter them again.

You can erase your roster, loaded report and standby history at any time using "Reset App" in the app's Settings tab. Uninstalling the app removes everything the app has stored, including its settings.

## The only information sent off your device

The app uses **OpenRouteService** (openrouteservice.org), an independent third-party service, to find places and work out travel time and distance. Two things are sent to it:

- **Addresses you type** when you add a location, so the service can find where that address is
- **The coordinates of two locations** when the app works out the driving distance and travel time between them

No names, hours, qualifications, or any other roster information is included in these requests. OpenRouteService's own handling of this data is governed by their own privacy policy, available at openrouteservice.org.

## Sharing standby details

The app lets you choose to share confirmed standby details (location, times, and the confirmed person's name) using your device's own share function, for example to send a text message or a message to a group chat. You start and control this. The app itself does not transmit this information anywhere, and has no involvement in the message once your device's share sheet opens.

## No advertising or analytics

The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

## Children's privacy

This app is a scheduling tool intended for adult use and is not directed at children.
