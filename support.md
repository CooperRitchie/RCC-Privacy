# Standby Roster - Support

Standby Roster (Standby Roster Calculator) helps teams allocate standby and relief shifts fairly. It is an independent tool by Cooper Ritchie - Ritchie Contracting & Consulting, and is not affiliated with any agency or organisation.

## Contact

Email **Cooper@RitchieCC.com.au** with questions, problems or suggestions. Please include your iPhone or iPad model, the iOS version and the app version (shown on the app's App Store page). Do not send real roster reports or personal details of crew members.

## Getting started

- **Try it first:** on the Standby tab, tap **Demo** to load a sample roster (all names are fictional).
- **Use your own roster:** tap the upload icon to import your hours report (Excel .xlsx), or tap **Create a new roster** to build one in the app.
- **Add a standby:** tap **Add standby**, enter the location, times, how many people are needed and any required qualifications, tick who has nominated, then tap **Calculate**.
- **Walkthrough:** Settings > Walkthrough runs a short guided tour using sample data.

## Common questions

**My report won't import.** The app expects a sheet with columns named Station, Date and Name, followed by the time-slot columns. Export the report again as .xlsx and try again. If it still fails, email the column headings (not the data).

**Travel times don't show.** Travel times need an internet connection and an address for both your station and the standby location (Settings > Station & roster, and My locations).

**Basic Mode or Memory Mode?** Basic Mode keeps nothing between sessions. Memory Mode keeps your roster and standbys on your device, with automatic backups in the Files app (On My iPhone > Standby Roster > Backups).

**How do I hand over to the next officer?** Use Settings > Handover & checking to share your roster and history; the next officer opens it in the app.

**How do I delete my data?** Settings > App settings > App mode > Reset App, then delete the Backups folder in the Files app - or uninstall the app. See the [privacy policy](./) for details.

## Privacy

Your roster stays on your device. There are no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. Read the full [privacy policy](./).
