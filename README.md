# Privacy Policy for Standby Roster Calculator

*Last updated: 21 October 2026*

Standby Roster Calculator ("the app") is an independent tool created by Cooper Ritchie - Ritchie Contracting & Consulting to help teams organise standby and relief shifts. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced by any agency or organisation.

## What information the app handles

Depending on how you use the app, it may handle the following information:

- Names of people on a roster, and their RR/RT hours, availability and allocation history, taken from a roster report you import or from a roster you create yourself
- Qualifications that you define, and which of them you assign to each person
- Standby details you enter, such as destination, start and end times, how many people are needed, and required qualifications
- A record of each standby decision: who nominated, who was selected, passed over or not eligible and why, any reason you type when picking someone other than the lowest, the rules profile in use, when the decision was made, and each nominee's RR hours at that time
- The name of your own station or unit, and the names and addresses of any locations you add
- Your chosen settings, such as your agency, your rules profile, Basic or Memory Mode, and light or dark appearance

The app does not use your location, camera, contacts or photos. The lists of stations, brigades and units built into the app are public information and say nothing about you.

## Where this information is stored

All of the information above is stored **only on your own device**, using standard local device storage. It is never uploaded to, or stored on, any server operated by the developer. The developer does not have access to this data, and it is not shared with the developer or any third party for analytics, marketing, or any other purpose.

If you use **Basic Mode**, your roster, report and standby information is not retained between sessions. If you use **Memory Mode**, this information is retained on your device between sessions until you clear it yourself. In both modes, app settings (such as your station, saved locations, qualification list and appearance) are kept on your device so you do not need to enter them again.

**Automatic backups (Memory Mode).** Each time a standby is confirmed, the app saves a copy of your roster and standby history as a handover file in its own folder on your device (in the Files app under On My iPhone or On My iPad > Standby Roster > Backups). Only the five most recent are kept. These files stay on your device unless you move or share them yourself.

Like other app data, the information above may be included in your device's own backups (for example iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer), if you have those turned on. Those backups are managed by Apple and by you, not by the developer.

## Deleting your data

- **Reset App** (Settings > App settings > App mode > Reset App) erases your roster, loaded report, standby history and settings, so the app starts again as if newly installed. It can also delete your saved locations and qualification list if you choose. It does not delete the automatic backups, so that you can still restore from one.
- **Automatic backups** can be deleted in the Files app: On My iPhone (or iPad) > Standby Roster > Backups.
- **Uninstalling the app** removes everything the app has stored on your device, including its backups.
- Copies you have shared yourself - for example a handover file or a standby message sent to someone - are outside the app, and need to be deleted wherever you sent them.

Because the developer never receives your data, there is nothing held by the developer to delete.

## The only information sent off your device

The app uses **OpenRouteService** (openrouteservice.org), an independent third-party service, to find places and work out travel time and distance. Two things are sent to it:

- **Addresses you type** when you add a location, so the service can find where that address is
- **The coordinates of two locations** when the app works out the driving distance and travel time between them

No names, hours, qualifications, or any other roster information is included in these requests. OpenRouteService's own handling of this data is governed by their own privacy policy, available at openrouteservice.org.

If you choose to open this privacy policy or to email the developer from within the app, your device opens your web browser or email app to do so.

## Sharing standby details and handover files

The app lets you choose to share confirmed standby details (location, times, and the confirmed person's name), a written record of a standby decision, a statistics spreadsheet, or a handover file, using your device's own share function - for example to send a message, an email, or a file to a group chat. You start and control this. The app itself does not transmit this information anywhere, and has no involvement once your device's share sheet opens.

## No advertising or analytics

The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

## Children's privacy

This app is a scheduling tool intended for adult use and is not directed at children.
